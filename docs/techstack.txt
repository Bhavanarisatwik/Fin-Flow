🧱 Core Stack

| Language       | **Dart**                  | Required for Flutter                           |

💾 How Your Data Will Be Stored

Your data flow will be:

Your App UI
     ↓
Hive Database
     ↓
Phone Internal Storage


So even if internet is OFF, your app works.

No servers
No Firebase
No backend
Fully personal

📦 Packages You Install in Antigravity

When you create Flutter project, add:

hive
hive_flutter
path_provider
provider


These are enough for most personal apps.

📱 How You Put the App in Your Phone

After building:

Run flutter build apk

APK file will be generated

Send APK to your phone

Install

Done. Your personal app is live.
